# Photo credits

All photographs are from Wikimedia Commons under licences that permit commercial reuse.
This file is the canonical attribution record: it lives in the repository, and the site
footer states that photographs are used under the licences recorded in the image credits.

Files live in `public/img/` and are served from the site root. Every one was reviewed by
eye before use. Two candidates were rejected after review:

- A civic street scene — a bank sign in frame and pedestrians at close range. A real
  person's face on a fictional government health site is not something to ship without
  releases.
- A construction hero — hazy, and under 1200px wide, so it would have looked soft
  behind text.

The shadow-AI desktop uses no third-party wallpaper, icon set or font: the wallpaper is
a generated gradient and the icons are text glyphs.
